# Shape: themes (the default)

The whole source's substance, grouped by idea rather than by where it appeared. It suits articles, essays, lectures, talks, and interviews, where the reader wants the gist in depth.

- `## TL;DR`: 3–5 sentences, the core message and why it matters.
- `## Key Points`: grouped under 3–6 **bold theme headings** you name from the content (not "Part 1"). Each bullet is a concrete claim or conclusion ("X beats Y for Z because…"), never "they discuss X", and ends with its anchor (see SKILL.md).
  - A tutorial or how-to adds a numbered `## Steps` section right after this one, each step anchored.
- `## Details Worth Knowing`: the specifics that didn't fit a theme, such as numbers, names, tools, examples, and caveats, each anchored.
- `## Bottom Line`: 1–3 sentences, what the source amounts to.
- `## Reader's note` (optional; see SKILL.md).
