# Shape: timeline

The source in its own order, one section per major movement. It suits courses, tutorials, long reports, multi-topic podcasts, and anything the reader will jump into rather than read end to end.

- `## TL;DR`: 3–5 sentences, the thesis and why it matters.
- **One `##` section per major movement, in the source's order**, each titled with a short name and its anchor: `## The AI timeline (11:25)` or `## Pricing (§3)`. For each section, give what is argued or taught, the strongest evidence or example given for it, and a direct quote only where the exact wording carries the point. Keep an anecdote only when it *is* the point.
  - A tutorial's section lists its steps, numbered.
- `## Bottom Line`: 2–3 sentences, what the source amounts to.
- `## Reader's note` (optional; see SKILL.md).
