# Shape: themes (the default)

The whole video's substance, grouped by idea rather than by when it was said. It suits keynotes, essays, lectures, and interviews, where the reader wants the gist in depth.

- `## TL;DR`: 3–5 sentences, the core message and why it matters. If the link carried a start time (`start_seconds`), add one line on what the video says there.
- `## Key Points`: grouped under 3–6 **bold theme headings** you name from the content (not "Part 1"). Each bullet is a concrete claim or conclusion ("X beats Y for Z because…"), never "they discuss X", and ends with its timestamp link.
  - A tutorial or how-to adds a numbered `## Steps` section right after this one, each step timestamped.
- `## Details Worth Knowing`: the specifics that didn't fit a theme, such as numbers, names, tools, examples, and caveats, each timestamped.
- `## Bottom Line`: 1–3 sentences, what the video amounts to.
- `## Reader's note` (optional; see SKILL.md).
