# Shape: timeline

The video in its own order, one section per major movement. It suits courses, tutorials, multi-topic podcasts, and anything the reader will jump into rather than read end to end.

- `## TL;DR`: 3–5 sentences, the thesis and why it matters. If the link carried a start time (`start_seconds`), add one line on what the video says there.
- **One `##` section per major movement, in the video's order**, each titled with a short name and its timestamp link: `## The AI timeline ([11:25](…&t=685s))`. For each section, give what is argued or taught, the strongest evidence or example given for it, and a direct quote only where the exact wording carries the point. Keep an anecdote only when it *is* the point.
  - A tutorial's section lists its steps, numbered.
- `## Bottom Line`: 2–3 sentences, what the video amounts to.
- `## Reader's note` (optional; see SKILL.md).
